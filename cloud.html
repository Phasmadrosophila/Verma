WHAT CLOUD ADDS

Everything Cloud adds.
Without breaking local trust.

You never have to give up offline ownership to get multi-device convenience.

01 / REMOTE SYNC

Sync beyond your living room.

Direct peer-to-peer QUIC sync works seamlessly on the same Wi-Fi. Verma Cloud adds an always-on encrypted relay on Cloudflare Workers, allowing your paired phone and laptop to sync across cellular data and remote networks without port forwarding. The relay only transports encrypted ciphertext blobs.

Explore direct sync
02 / BROWSER VAULT

Your vault in any modern browser.

Need to grab an API key or recovery code while away from your primary workstation? Log into web.verma.cloud. Client-side libsodium decrypts your vault directly in your browser tab. Your master password and plaintext secrets are never seen by the server.

Open Web Vault
03 / CONTINUITY

Emergency access that doesn't sleep.

Store a pre-encrypted emergency recovery package in the cloud relay. If you fail to check in after your defined grace period (e.g., 90 days), the service automatically alerts you, and if unacknowledged, safely delivers the package to your designated trusted recipient.

Read emergency spec
SANDBOXED CLOUD COPILOT

Cloud AI triage.
With an unbreakable line.
