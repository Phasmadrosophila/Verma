GOOD QUESTIONS, SIMPLE ANSWERS

A little more clarity.

What can I keep in Verma?

The concept brings logins, private notes, and API keys together in one vault. Try it with the included sample items, or add your own fictional examples.

Do I need an account to try it?

No account or download is needed. Open the demo, explore the welcome screens, and create a sample vault. You can also choose “Explore demo” to jump into the sample items.

Can the helper see my passwords?

The demo’s search only uses titles, websites, and tags. It does not search passwords, private note contents, or keys. You can turn the helper off and continue using regular search.

Can I bring passwords from another app?

You can explore that flow with a sample import: review suggested tags, decide how to handle a duplicate, and confirm what gets added. Importing real files is not part of this design concept.

Is this ready for my real passwords?

This is an interactive design concept, so please use sample values only. Data stays in the current page session and resets on reload. Encryption, real recovery, AI, and device synchronization are not implemented.
