GOOD QUESTIONS, SIMPLE ANSWERS

A little more clarity.

What can I store in Verma?

Verma securely stores account logins, private notes, and developer API keys all in one unified, encrypted local vault.

Do I need to create a third-party account?

No. Verma is 100% local-first and serverless. You don’t need an email, phone number, or centralized account. Your vault belongs entirely to you and stays on your hardware.

Can the AI assistant see my passwords?

Never. Secret fields like passwords, private keys, and note contents are strictly blocked by trusted application redaction before the AI model ever sees a prompt. Furthermore, the local AI runtime operates in a sandboxed process with network access disabled.

How do I migrate from Chrome, 1Password, or Bitwarden?

Verma includes a Smart CSV Import tool. It automatically maps messy browser export columns, tags services, detects duplicate entries, and lets you review every single record before adding it to your vault.

How are my secrets encrypted and synced?

Your vault is secured with industry-standard libsodium encryption and protected with a 24-word recovery phrase. Multi-device sync uses authenticated direct device-to-device QUIC connections over your local Wi-Fi, without routing your secret data through central company servers.
