### CB-01: `ollama.ts` missing loopback validation
- **File:** `apps/api/src/ai/ollama.ts:36`
- **Issue:** `askYourVaultOllama` builds a `fetch` URL from `process.env.OLLAMA_URL` with no host validation. `AiAdapter` in `adapter.ts:37` correctly calls `isLocalUrl()`, but `ollama.ts` does not. - **Evidence:** `packages/shared/src/sync/pairing.ts:84`
