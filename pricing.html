Why is Verma Local completely free?

Localhost belongs to you. Verma Local runs 100% on your own machines and syncs directly between your devices over local Wi-Fi. Because there are no centralized servers hosting your database, there are no ongoing cloud infrastructure costs for us to pass onto you. We believe digital privacy is a fundamental standard.

What is the difference between Basic User and Verma Local?

Basic User is a lightweight starter tier with 1 vault account on a single personal device. Verma Local is our full open-source community edition that unlocks unlimited accounts, on-device sandboxed AI assistance, direct peer-to-peer QUIC device sync, and self-hosted Docker relay containers.

Can Verma Cloud ever read my passwords?

Never. Verma Cloud is an opaque, blind relay. All entries and metadata are encrypted client-side on your device using libsodium before transmission. Our relay only handles blind encrypted packets and cannot decrypt your data.

Do I need to provide a credit card or email to get started?

No. Both Basic User and Verma Local require no email, no registration, and no payment information. Just install and your vault is ready.
